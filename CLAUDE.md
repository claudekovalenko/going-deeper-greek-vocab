# Working with this user

**Be brief.** Short answers, numbered steps, no long explanations. They have
asked for this explicitly. Do not pad replies with rationale, caveats or
recaps — state what was done or what to do, and stop.

## Mnemonic rewrites

A word marked Replace with **no note** is not a puzzle to ask about. It means
"I don't like it, use your own judgement" — write a genuinely different and
better hook, and never ask what they wanted.
